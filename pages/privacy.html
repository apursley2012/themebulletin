Privacy and third-party services

Overview

ThemeBulletin is a publicly accessible static portfolio directory. It does not require an account, payment, or personal information to browse.

Information you provide

The website does not include a contact form, newsletter signup, or account registration. If you contact the project owner through a linked third-party service, that service handles the information you choose to provide.

Analytics, cookies, and storage

The delivered website does not implement its own analytics, advertising pixels, tracking cookies, or browser-storage profiling. Third-party services used to host the site, serve fonts, or display linked projects may process connection information under their own policies.

External services

The site loads typefaces from Google Fonts and links to GitHub and independently hosted portfolio demos. Requests to these services can disclose technical information such as an IP address and browser details to their operators. Their policies apply when you use their services.

Changes and questions

This notice describes the website source as provided in November 2026. If site integrations change, the notice should be reviewed. For questions, use the project repository link in the footer.
