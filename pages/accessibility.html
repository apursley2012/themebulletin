Accessibility

Accessibility features, design limitations, and feedback for ThemeBulletin.

Site information

Accessibility statement

Approach

ThemeBulletin aims to provide navigable semantic pages, visible keyboard focus, a skip link, descriptive page titles, and text alternatives for theme previews.

Known design constraint

The corkboard preserves its square composition and scales uniformly to fit narrower viewports. The pinned items scale with the board, which may make small text harder to read on narrow screens.

Testing status

The presence of accessibility features in the source does not establish compliance with WCAG or guarantee compatibility with every browser, screen reader, or device. A complete assistive-technology audit has not been certified.

Feedback

If a page or interaction creates an accessibility barrier, report the affected URL and the problem through the project repository.
